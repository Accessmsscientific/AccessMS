Applications

Built for the questions biomedical researchers ask.

From global discovery proteomics to targeted quantitation of a single compound, we support projects across the life sciences.

Proteomics

Global protein identification and quantitation across cells, tissues and biofluids.

Post-translational modifications

Phosphoproteomics, glycosylation and other PTMs, with site-level localization.

Metabolomics

Targeted and untargeted profiling of small-molecule metabolites.

RNA modifications

Detection and quantitation of modified nucleosides by LC-MS.

Biomarker analysis

Discovery, verification and targeted panels for translational studies.

Drug discovery

Target engagement, mechanism of action and compound characterization.

Toxicology

Quantitation of drugs, metabolites and exposures in complex matrices.

Small-molecule analysis

Accurate-mass identification and quantitative small-molecule assays.

Why Access MS

More than a data file at the end of a queue.

Many providers stop at the data file. We stay with the project, from shaping the design up front to supporting you through peer review.

  • The right platform for each projectWe are not tied to one vendor or instrument, and route each study to the best-suited platform, in-house or through trusted partners.
  • Quality control at every stepQC samples, standards and performance monitoring are built in, and reported back to you.
  • Results you can publishFigures, methods and repository deposition prepared to journal standards.
