About us

Mass spectrometry scientists, working on your side.

Access MS Scientific was founded by a team of mass spectrometry experts from the University of Toronto to make advanced MS accessible to every research group, whether or not it has a mass spectrometer down the hall.

Who we are

A partner, not a queue.

Based in Toronto, Ontario, we provide consultation for all MS-based research needs: experimental design, access to current-generation mass spectrometry, and a complete analysis pipeline.

Our team has hands-on experience across proteomics, post-translational modifications, metabolomics, RNA modifications, drug discovery, biomarker analysis and toxicology. That breadth lets us see a project from every angle: the chemistry, the biology and the statistics.
