In detail

What each stage includes.

Deliverables are agreed in writing at the planning stage, so you always know what you will receive and when.

Stage 01

Initial consultation

We start with your research question, not with an instrument.

A scientist-to-scientist conversation about your hypothesis, sample types, timelines and budget. We tell you candidly what mass spectrometry can and cannot answer for your project.

  • Review of your research question and existing data
  • Feasibility and sample-size discussion
  • Recommended analytical strategy and scope
Stage 02

Experimental project plan

A written plan you can take to your lab, your PI or your grant.

We design the study around statistical power and the biology you care about, then match it to the most suitable acquisition strategy and platform.

  • Study design, controls and randomization
  • Choice of quantitation strategy (label-free, labelled or targeted)
  • Sample submission guide and timeline
Stage 03

Sample preparation

Robust, reproducible preparation is where good data begins.

Protocols are optimized for your matrix — cells, tissue, plasma or serum, biofluids or purified material — with QC samples and standards built in from the start.

  • Extraction, digestion and clean-up
  • Enrichment for post-translational modifications
  • Process controls and pooled QC samples
Stage 04

Data generation

Current-generation LC-MS, matched to the question.

Your samples are acquired on the platform best suited to the project, either in-house or through trusted partner facilities, with performance monitored throughout every run.

  • Discovery (DDA / DIA) and targeted acquisition
  • System-suitability and QC monitoring
  • Raw data and acquisition metadata delivered
Stage 05

Data analysis

From raw spectra to statistically sound biological insight.

Identification, quantitation and statistics are performed with established, documented pipelines, then integrated with pathways, networks and your other data.

  • Identification, quantitation and QC reporting
  • Differential, multivariate and network analysis
  • Interactive review of results with your team
Stage 06

Pre-publication services

Support until the paper is out the door.

We help you present the work clearly and meet journal and repository requirements, and stay available through peer review.

  • Publication-quality figures and tables
  • Methods text and supplementary data
  • Repository deposition and reviewer responses

Applications

Areas of expertise.

Proteomics, metabolomics and beyond, applied wherever mass spectrometry can move the research forward.

Proteomics

Global protein identification and quantitation across cells, tissues and biofluids.

Post-translational modifications

Phosphoproteomics, glycosylation and other PTMs, with site-level localization.

Metabolomics

Targeted and untargeted profiling of small-molecule metabolites.

RNA modifications

Detection and quantitation of modified nucleosides by LC-MS.

Biomarker analysis

Discovery, verification and targeted panels for translational studies.

Drug discovery

Target engagement, mechanism of action and compound characterization.

Toxicology

Quantitation of drugs, metabolites and exposures in complex matrices.

Small-molecule analysis

Accurate-mass identification and quantitative small-molecule assays.
